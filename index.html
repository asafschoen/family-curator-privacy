🔒 The Family Curator Privacy Pledge

Your family memories, photos, and biometric data NEVER leave your physical device. Family Curator operates as an airgapped on-device AI gatekeeper. We do not have servers, user accounts, databases in the cloud, or analytics trackers. Zero bytes of personal or biometric data are transmitted over the internet.

📡 1. Zero Network Calls & No Internet Permission

Family Curator does NOT declare, request, or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android application manifest (AndroidManifest.xml).

Because the application completely lacks network permissions at the operating system level, it is physically and technically impossible for the app to:

  • Upload, stream, or synchronize your images or videos to any remote server or cloud service.
  • Transmit facial crops, biometric embeddings, or profile identities off your device.
  • Collect crash telemetry, usage analytics, device identifiers, or tracking metrics.
  • Display network advertisements or connect to third-party ad networks.

You can verify this in Android Settings > Apps > Family Curator > Permissions, where Internet access is completely absent.

🧠 2. On-Device Biometric Face Recognition

Family Curator utilizes lightweight on-device machine learning models (MobileFaceNet and Face Landmark Alignment) to detect and group family members:

  • Local Inferences Only: All face detection, 5-point landmark eye alignment, and 192-dimensional floating-point embedding computations are executed 100% on-device inside background Dart Isolates and native threads.
  • Encapsulated Sandbox Storage: Reference embeddings and aligned 112x112 pixel face crops are stored strictly in the application’s private SQLite database (family_curator.db) within internal app sandbox storage.
  • No Cloud AI APIs: The application does not interface with any cloud vision APIs, external model hosts, or remote machine learning services.

📂 3. Scoped Storage Compliance & Copy-Only Strategy

To rescue family memories buried in WhatsApp chat folders, Family Curator requests standard granular Android media permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES and READ_MEDIA_VIDEO on Android 13+ / API 33+; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on earlier versions):

  • Strictly Read & Copy: Family Curator operates on a non-destructive Copy-Only policy. Approved photos and videos are copied to Pictures/FamilyCurator/.
  • WhatsApp Media is Untouched: The app NEVER deletes, moves, renames, alters, or modifies original files located in WhatsApp media folders (e.g., Android/media/com.whatsapp/... or WhatsApp/Media/...).
  • No Full Storage Access: The app does NOT request or require MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.
  • EXIF Capture Date Repair: When copying files, the app inspects WhatsApp filename timestamps and populates standard EXIF capture date tags (TAG_DATETIME_ORIGINAL) on the destination copy so memories sort chronologically in your gallery.

🔄 4. Foreground Service Justification (Data Sync)

Family Curator declares the FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC permission to perform continuous offline WhatsApp media backlog scanning in the background:

  • User-Initiated: The background scanning service only starts when you tap "Start Continuous Scan".
  • Persistent Visibility: While active, the service displays a mandatory, non-dismissible notification stating "Scanning WhatsApp Media..." with real-time percentage progress.
  • Immediate Control: The notification contains direct, interactive Pause and Stop action buttons allowing you to halt processing at any moment.
  • 100% Offline: No network synchronization occurs during foreground service execution.

💳 5. In-App Purchases & Google Play Billing

Family Curator offers an optional one-time lifetime Pro unlock (family_curator_pro_lifetime) via the official Google Play Billing library:

  • All billing transactions are handled exclusively through secure local inter-process communication (IPC) with the Google Play Store app.
  • Family Curator does not collect, receive, or store your payment details, credit card numbers, billing addresses, or Google account credentials.
  • Pro entitlement status is cached locally in the on-device SQLite database.

🗑️ 6. Data Retention, Portability & Deletion Rights

Because Family Curator has no cloud servers or external accounts, you maintain complete, sovereign control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Clear: You can purge reference faces, reset scanned history, or delete curated backups at any time directly in Settings > Storage & Maintenance.
  • Complete App Removal: Uninstalling Family Curator permanently and immediately obliterates all face embeddings, profile metadata, and local database tables from your device.
